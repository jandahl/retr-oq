    About BeOS

    BeOS R5 Personal Edition

    A Tracker / Deskbar prototype — yellow title tabs, a Deskbar parked in the upper right, Swiss chrome around original icons. The way a BeBox actually looked in 2000, minus the trademarked leaf.

    Double-click a tab to hide the window. Zoom (the square) grows it. The Deskbar lists whatever is running.

    Yellow-tab slant adapted from NovusGFX/retro-design-system (MIT). Palette is R5 BeOS Blue, not theirs. Font is TeX Gyre Heros (GFL).
